Sponsor & Monetize

Turn traffic into sponsorship revenue.

This site can generate income through banner ads, featured sponsorships, station promotions, affiliate recommendations, and partnerships with radio brands and local businesses.

Ways to Earn

Offer paid placement on the homepage, category pages, or individual station cards. You can also sell featured “Sponsored Stations” sections and seasonal campaign banners.

Ad Network Setup

Connect Google AdSense or similar ad networks to start displaying banner ads and in-page units across the site.

Affiliate Revenue

Add affiliate links for audio gear, streaming apps, headphones, and related products that your audience is likely to buy.

Next Steps

Set up a business email, create a pricing sheet, and add a simple sponsor request form or contact route so brands can book placements quickly.
